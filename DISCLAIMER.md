## 3. Acknowledgment of Model Fallibility
Users must recognize that the deep learning neural networks embedded within Aakhi operate on probabilistic estimations derived from training datasets. Consequently:
- **The application might make mistakes.**
- The models may produce **false positives** (flagging healthy tissue as anomalous) or **false negatives** (failing to identify subtle or atypical pathological indicators).
- Artifacts in fundus photographs—such as poor lighting, sensor dust, cataracts, or off-center alignment—can significantly degrade model precision and introduce erroneous predictions.

## 4. Recommended System Specifications
Deep learning computer vision models involve highly intensive matrix computations. To ensure stable execution, minimize inference bottleneck delays, and prevent system resource exhaustion, we strongly recommend running Aakhi on hardware meeting or exceeding the following specifications:

- **Graphics Processing Unit (GPU)**: Dedicated **NVIDIA GPU with 4 GB VRAM or more** (supporting CUDA acceleration).
- **System Memory (RAM)**: Minimum **16 GB RAM** recommended.
- **Operating System**: Windows 10 / Windows 11 (64-bit).

> **Note on CPU-Only Deployments**: When utilizing the pre-bundled portable standalone `.exe` distribution without GPU runtime support, inference will fall back entirely to system CPU threads. Under CPU execution, analysis processing times are expected to take longer (typically 1 to 4 minutes per image scan).
